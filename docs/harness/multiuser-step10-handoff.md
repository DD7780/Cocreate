A one-time working-set trim increased measured free RAM from 1,304 to 1,605 MiB without closing applications or changing settings; the published [record](../../artifacts/multiuser-step10/memory-reclaimed.json) omits process identities. A later quota miss still occurred, so RAM alone is not established as its cause. The native launcher now reports bounded user/kernel 100 ns ticks, process/limit-termination counts and wall milliseconds alongside peak memory/exit status. All policy limits remain unchanged. [Microsoft's accounting contract](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winnt/ns-winnt-jobobject_basic_accounting_information) counts associations including rejected processes; it is not an instantaneous active-process count. The strengthened CPU adversity check verifies measured user-time enforcement and cleanup without treating that cumulative count as one active process.
